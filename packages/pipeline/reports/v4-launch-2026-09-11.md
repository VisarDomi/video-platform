## Changes tested

- Native classification remains based on coded pixel count >= 1920×1080.
- Pure qualifying material is remuxed; mixed material with >=90% qualifying
  EXTINF duration retains only qualifying segments and remuxes; otherwise all
  segments are converted. Original source playlists are not rewritten.
- Production conversion preserves aspect without padding/cropping and meets
  both oriented Full-HD edges. 4:3 targets 1920×1440; 5:4 targets 1920×1536.
  Standard landscape/portrait remain 1920×1080 / 1080×1920. Wider custom
  aspects retain at least a 1080-pixel short edge.
- Verification reads the actual advertised HLS master and requires the
  Full-HD pixel tier, not a title label or merely an edit-page direct link.
  A 0.5% pixel tolerance accommodates provider dimension rounding.
- Generic pre-transfer upload failures and login failures retain a durable
  next-day retry. Captcha backoff is capped at one day. Manual pauses remain
  manual; verification continues independently during upload cooldowns.
- Missing IDs remain acceptance-unknown. Current-generation diagnostic-title
  matches are searched daily, requiring exactly one match. No blind reupload,
  natural-title ownership guess, or adoption of an older generation occurs.
- Verification/authentication/network errors postpone confirmation by 24h
  instead of repeatedly logging in every 30 seconds or declaring absence.
- Submission-page evidence and verification renditions/retry reasons are
  saved in the upload ledger and exposed in comparison.json. Duplicate and
  rejection text is retained as evidence, not treated as permission to reupload.
