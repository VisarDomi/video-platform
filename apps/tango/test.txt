Features:

1. Home
- Opening Home displays a scrollable list of live streamers.
- The list is followed + recommended.
- The list appears as soon as the streams are available.
- Streamer names and other details may improve afterward without delaying the
  list.
- Refreshing Home fetches a fresh list.
- Selecting a streamer navigates to that streamer's own URL.

2. Stream
- A stream URL displays its stream together with the custom stream UI.
- Stream playback starts as soon as its media is available.
- Streamer names and other details may improve afterward without delaying
  playback.
- The current stream and its adjacent streams are loaded for vertical
  navigation.
- Refreshing a stream URL fetches a fresh list and finds the current streamer
  within it.
- If the current streamer is no longer available, the first streamer in the
  fresh list becomes current.

3. Stream navigation
- Dragging vertically reveals the adjacent stream in the direction of travel.
- Releasing after a sufficient drag completes the move to the adjacent stream.
- Releasing a short drag returns to the current stream.
- The correct previous and next streams are loaded around the current stream.
- Moving to another stream changes the URL without adding another Back step.
- Discovered costreamers enter the stream order at the appropriate position
  and may become the next or previous stream.

4. Back to Home
- Safari's left-edge Back gesture returns from a stream to Home.
- Home returns with the list updated by what was discovered while viewing
  streams.
- The last viewed streamer is highlighted.

5. Stream UI
- A newly selected stream starts muted.
- The mute control toggles audio for the current stream and reflects its state.
- Horizontal gestures show and hide the stream controls.
- The current stream displays the best available streamer name.
- Audio-only streams are removed from the available streams.

6. Stream actions
- Follow and unfollow update the current stream's visible following state.
- Blocking requires confirmation.
- Blocking a followed streamer also unfollows it.
- A successfully blocked streamer is removed from the available streams.
- The download-list control adds or removes the current streamer and reflects
  the resulting membership.
- A stream that becomes unavailable is removed from navigation.
