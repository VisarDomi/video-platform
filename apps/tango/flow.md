Flows:
1. open home - see a list of followed and recommended - can scroll through it - refresh the page to get a fresher list
2. click a stream from this list - navigate natively to the stream url
3. on stream url a stream is shown along with the custom ui and custom swipe gestures like tiktok.
4. swipe up/down to get to the next (preloaded) stream and change the URL without adding another Back step
5. discovered costreamers are appended to the end, without duplicating or moving existing streamers; navigation keeps this list until Home is refreshed
6. refreshing a stream page keeps the tab's list; the list is fetched only when the tab has none
7. a stream that errors or plays without a picture leaves the list and the next one takes its place (the first one if it was never listed)
8. the streams before and after the current one are loaded
9. follow, a two-step block and the PC download-list +/- act on the current stream; a blocked stream leaves the list
10. swipe back brings us back to the updated list, with the last viewed streamer highlighted
